How stock checks work

Stock check schedule. Checks run in batches every 10 minutes, with at least 20 minutes between scans of the same seller. A listing is eligible for another check after about an hour. Seller cooldowns and the queue can make that longer. “Latest stock reading” is the newest check across the whole catalog, and each radio card shows its own check time.

In stock is a seller’s stock report. Orderable means the seller accepts orders, but shelf stock is unconfirmed. Unverified includes listings awaiting a check, seller pauses, and older or inconclusive readings. Historical prices are marked.

Manual review listings, marked with an asterisk, are from sellers that block automated checks, such as Etsy, DigiKey and Mouser. Their price and stock are entered by hand, so they can lag the seller’s page. Use the Manual review tab to see them together.

Both firmware options means the hardware supports either firmware by selection or flashing. “Only” reflects what is documented for the tracked listing. Check the seller’s selected variant, frequency, shipping, and final price before ordering.
