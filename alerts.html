Phone alerts

Get a notification on your phone when a radio comes back in stock or its price drops. It is free, there is no account to make and no email to give, and it works through ntfy, a free, open-source notification app.

Phone alerts are not turned on for this site yet. The steps below show how they will work. In the meantime you can follow changes with the RSS feed.

  1. 1Install the ntfy app

    ntfy is a separate app made by Philipp Heckel, not by this site. Look for the one called just ntfy.

    Android without Google Play: ntfy is also on F-Droid (opens in a new tab). No phone handy? You can read the alerts in a browser instead, from the Open in your browser link in step 2.

  2. 2Follow all alerts

    In the app, tap the + button, type this topic name, and tap Subscribe. Leave the server as the default, ntfy.sh.

    Loading… Open in your browser (opens in a new tab)

    That is all. The next restock or price drop on any radio in the catalog arrives on your phone.

  3. 3Or follow only some radios

    Too many alerts? Every radio has its own topic. Star the radios you care about on the catalog, open My picks, and choose Phone alerts for this radio on a pick. It opens this page with that radio's topic, ready to subscribe to.

    Open in your browser (opens in a new tab)

What an alert looks like

ntfy · example

LILYGO T-Deck at Rokland dropped from $74.95 to $67.97

Confirm stock and the selected variant with the seller.

  • Tap the alert and it opens that radio's page at the seller.
  • Restocks arrive as high priority, so they stand out. Price drops arrive as normal ones.
  • Several changes at once arrive as one message, not a pile of them.
  • Not instant to the second. Stock is checked about once an hour, so an alert can lag a little behind the seller's page.

Good to know

  • The topic is public. Anyone who knows the name can read it, and anyone could also post to it, so only trust an alert after you check the seller's own page. This site posts public facts only: what changed, which radio, which seller.
  • We do not know who follows. There are no accounts and no sign-ups here. The ntfy service sees your phone's address the way any online service does.
  • To stop alerts, open the topic in the ntfy app and choose to unsubscribe, or mute it from the app's menu.
  • Not affiliated. ntfy is its own project. This site only posts to it.
  • Prefer a feed reader? The RSS feed carries the same changes, and My picks has a personal feed for just your radios.
